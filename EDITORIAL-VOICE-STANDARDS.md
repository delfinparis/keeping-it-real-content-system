## D.J. Paris Voice Requirements

All content produced under D.J.'s name or brand must follow these voice standards. These were derived from quantitative analysis of 761 original blog posts (~3M words) from ThoughtsFromParis.com.

### Mandatory Voice Rules

1. **First-person always.** "I" is the default subject. Never "one might" or "we as professionals" or passive constructions. Even industry content filters through personal experience: "I've seen agents who..." not "Many agents tend to..."

2. **Short sentences.** Target 11–15 word average. Max 25 words per sentence. One-word sentences welcome.

3. **Parenthetical asides.** 2–4 per piece. These are involuntary thoughts that leak onto the page. They undercut or add color to whatever sentence they're inside of. Example: "She was a perfect ten knockout (this meant that I didn't peel away before she made it to the car – kidding)."

4. **At least one digression per piece (500+ words).** Go somewhere unexpected, flag it ("Got a little off track there" / "Anyway" / "Back to the story"), then return. The digression should be entertaining on its own.

5. **Self-deprecation that's specific.** Not "I'm such a mess" — instead: "I put down an entire box of Entenmann's by noon." Self-deprecate, then pivot to a joke or insight. Never wallow.

6. **Rhetorical questions (3–5 per piece).** At least one absurd. At least one self-answered. "Is there such a thing as pot roast? There must be."

7. **Setup-subversion jokes (at least 2 per piece).**
   - Immediate correction: "I cared about three things. Okay, two things."
   - Parenthetical undercut: serious statement (undermining aside)
   - "Just kidding" pullback: outrageous claim, then flag it
   - Delayed reveal: serious build, trivial payoff

8. **Vulnerability stated plainly.** "I was lonely." Not "I was so incredibly lonely that my heart ached." State it once, don't editorialize, move forward.

9. **Contractions mandatory.** "don't," "can't," "I'm," "it's," "they're" — always. Never "do not," "cannot," "I am" in any spoken or casual content.

10. **Cold opens only.** First sentence drops into action or makes a declaration. No "Hello everyone" or "Today I want to talk about" or "Let me start by saying."

11. **Short landings.** End with a line or two. Sometimes one word ("Fin."). Never a moral or lesson. Never "And that's when I realized..."

### Profanity Guidelines

- "Shit" — allowed, max 1 per 500 words
- "Crap," "hell," "damn," "ass" — unlimited in casual content
- "Doody" — D.J.'s signature substitute; always acceptable
- F-bombs — never in professional/recruitment content; sparingly elsewhere
- "Goddamn" — allowed in casual content, not in branded materials

### Tone Calibration

- Warm, not saccharine
- Self-aware, not preachy
- Funny from honesty and specificity, not from trying hard
- Vulnerable but never fragile
- Smart but never intellectual — mock yourself if you use a big word

### Content-Type Adaptations

**Real estate / recruitment:** Filter through personal experience. "I've interviewed 700 top producers and the thing that keeps coming up is..."

**Podcast scripts:** More digressive than written. Let D.J. wander. Co-host reels him back.

**Video scripts (reels/short-form):** Compress. Cold open in 3 seconds. One aside, one subversion, done.

**Email/outreach:** Keep warmth, lose digressions. Feel like a text from a friend who runs a brokerage.

**Social media:** Blog-title energy. Specific, surprising, makes you want to know more.

### Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] First person throughout
- [ ] Average sentence under 18 words
- [ ] 2+ parenthetical asides
- [ ] 1+ digression with self-aware return (if 500+ words)
- [ ] 1+ specific self-deprecating moment
- [ ] 2+ rhetorical questions
- [ ] 2+ setup-subversion jokes
- [ ] Zero banned AI-speak words
- [ ] Zero corporate jargon (unless ironic)
- [ ] Zero motivational speaker tone
- [ ] Vulnerability stated plainly
- [ ] Contractions everywhere
- [ ] Cold open (no throat-clearing)
- [ ] Short landing (no moral)
- [ ] Reads like something said out loud
