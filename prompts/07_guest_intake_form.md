### 3. What Should D.J. Ask You About?
This is the most important one. D.J. does his own research on every guest, but nobody knows your story better than you.

**What are you most excited to talk about right now?** Maybe it's a system that's working, a lesson you learned the hard way, something you just changed in your business, or a strong opinion you have about the industry. Just give us 2-3 bullet points - even a sentence each is great.

### 7. Go Live During Our Recording (Optional but Encouraged!)
Want to double your exposure from this episode? We encourage guests to **go live on their own social media while we record.** Your audience gets to watch a real podcast interview happen in real time - and it drives listeners to the finished episode.

**How it works:**
1. **Download Restream** (free plan works): [restream.io](https://restream.io)
2. **Connect your Facebook and/or Instagram** to Restream before recording day
3. **A few minutes before we start**, open Restream and go live — it will simultaneously stream to whichever platforms you've connected
4. **Title suggestion:** "I'm live on the Keeping It Real Podcast with D.J. Paris!"
5. **When we wrap**, just end your Restream - that's it

**Why this is great for you:**
- Your followers see you being interviewed on a major podcast (instant credibility)
- The live stream stays on your profile as a video your audience can watch later
- You'll pick up new followers from our audience who find you through the episode

**Totally optional** - if you'd rather not, no worries at all. The episode will be great either way. But guests who go live tell us they love the extra engagement it creates.

## INTERNAL NOTES (for Zana — do not send to guest)

**When to send:** Within 24 hours of confirming the booking. Follow up 3 days before recording if no response.

**What to do with responses:**
1. Save the bio exactly as sent — D.J. reads it verbatim
2. Go to the Interview Prep web app and paste in the guest's responses
3. Upload any PDFs, headshots, or attachments the guest sent
4. Click "Generate Interview Prep" — the app will research the guest and produce a full PDF
5. Download the PDF and attach it to the guest's Trello card
6. Flag anything in "off-limits" to D.J. directly

**If the guest doesn't respond:** That's fine. D.J.'s research process doesn't depend on this. But the bio and social links are the minimum — follow up for at least those.

**If the guest sends a 2-page bio:** Use it. D.J. reads whatever they send. If it's genuinely too long (3+ minutes to read aloud), Zana can reply: "This is great! Just so you know, D.J. reads the bio word for word on air — would you like to trim it to about 30 seconds of reading time, or are you happy with the full version?"

**If the guest asks "What questions will D.J. ask?":** Reply: "D.J. does custom research for every guest, so the exact questions will be tailored to you. But I can tell you the conversation will focus on [their bullet points from #3] and will be very conversational — not scripted. Just bring your expertise and D.J. will guide the conversation."

*Never send the full question list in advance. It kills spontaneity.*
