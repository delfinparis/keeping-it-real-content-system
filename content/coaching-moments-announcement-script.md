# Coaching Moments with Mike Opyd — Announcement Episode Script

## Mike Opyd Bio

**Mike Opyd** is a Chicago-based real estate coach, top-producing Realtor, published author, and former brokerage owner. In 2009, Mike left a corporate finance job at State Street Corporation in Boston, moved back to Chicago, and dove headfirst into real estate with just $2,000 in his pocket — during the Great Recession. No safety net. No plan B.

The early years were brutal. Mike didn't close his first sale until year three. But he kept grinding, developed a repeatable system, and built himself into a top producer recognized by the Chicago Association of Realtors for both total sales volume and total transactions. His lifetime sales exceed $75 million.

In 2016, Mike opened his own brokerage in Chicago's West Loop, and in 2017 he co-founded RE/MAX NEXT with partner Rafay Qamar — a brokerage designed to give agents the education and tools they actually need to succeed. Under Mike's mentorship, RE/MAX NEXT grew to multiple offices producing $200 million in annual sales, with new agents going from zero production to over $1 million in their first 12 months.

Mike is also the author of *The Education of a Real Estate Agent* and its companion workbook. His insights have been featured in Forbes, Inman, US News, Investopedia, the Chicago Tribune, and the Chicago Sun-Times.

Today, Mike runs his coaching and consulting practice full-time, helping agents who feel stuck break through to the next level using his 5-Step formula — the same system he used to build his own career from nothing.
